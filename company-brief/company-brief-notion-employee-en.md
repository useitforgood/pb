# Notion — Pre-Employment Intelligence Brief

_Date: 2026-06-20_

## Company Overview

Notion is an AI-powered all-in-one workspace combining documents, wikis, project management, and custom AI agents into a single platform. Founded in 2013, it has grown to over 100 million users worldwide and is used by 62% of Fortune 100 companies. The product competes with Confluence, Coda, Google Docs, and Asana, positioning itself as a replacement for fragmented productivity tool stacks.

## Background

- **Founded:** 2013, San Francisco (Ivan Zhao, Akshay Kothari, Chris Prucha, Jessica Lam, Simon Last, Toby Schachman)
- **Headquarters:** San Francisco, CA (with offices in New York, Dublin, Tokyo, Seoul, Sydney, Hyderabad, Munich, Paris)
- **Valuation:** $10B (October 2021 Series D) — some sources report $11B as of December 2025
- **Total funding:** $418M over 9 rounds. Key investors: Sequoia Capital, Coatue Management, Index Ventures, Accel, First Round Capital
- **Revenue:** $500M ARR (September 2025); Q1 2026 revenue grew 64% YoY. Debt-free, cash-rich
- **Users:** 100M+ worldwide; 62% of Fortune 100; 98% of Forbes Cloud 100; 50%+ of Y Combinator companies
- **Stage:** Late-stage private (Series D). Bloomberg reported a possible IPO as early as late 2026

## Team & Scale

- **Employees:** ~1,000 full-time (Latka, 2026) — other sources report much higher numbers (4.7K per LeadIQ, 6K per Tracxn), likely including contractors, community, or different counting methodology. The ~1K figure aligns with Notion's historically lean team
- **Trend:** Growing. Headcount up from ~808 in 2024 to ~1K in 2026. Revenue growing 64% YoY suggests continued hiring
- **Open positions:** 150+ roles, heavily weighted toward Sales (~60), Engineering (~15), Customer Success (~15)
- **Recent changes:** No confirmed major layoffs, though Blind discussions (May 2026) reference some workforce changes. Talent attrition to AI labs and Cursor noted by employees. Notion launched Developer Platform (May 2026), Mail (April 2025), and v3.0 with AI agents (September 2025)

## What They Actually Build

Notion's core product is a block-based workspace where teams build custom wikis, project trackers, and documents. The engineering focus has shifted heavily toward AI: custom agents, enterprise search, AI meeting notes, and integrations with external tools (Slack, Gmail, GitHub). The team is relatively small for the user base, meaning engineers own large surface areas. Job postings emphasize craft, speed, and high ownership. Expect to ship features to 100M+ users with a lean team — high leverage, high pressure.

## Culture & Values

**What the company says:** Four core values: "Customer in every room," "Own the outcome" (no passengers — hire for agency), "Why not today" (bias toward action), "Direct and kind" (directness is kindness — say the hard thing). The company draws inspiration from computing pioneers like Alan Kay and Doug Engelbart.

**What employees say:**
- High craft, low ego — collaborative and genuinely passionate team. "Perfect combination of fast pace, high craft, and low ego"
- Approachable leadership and a culture that encourages learning and growth
- Fast-paced environment leads to burnout and churn; priorities shift quickly and strategy isn't always well-communicated
- **Discrepancies with official messaging:** "Own the outcome" culture is real but can tip into overwork. Great talent is leaving for AI labs and Cursor — the "why not today" urgency can feel unsustainable

## Green Flags

- Exceptional Glassdoor ratings (4.3/5) and 3 consecutive Best Places to Work awards — rare for a company this size
- Revenue growing 64% YoY with strong margins and no debt — financially healthy and not dependent on new funding
- Lean team serving 100M+ users means high individual impact and leverage
- Possible IPO in late 2026 — equity could be very valuable if timing works out

## Red Flags

- Management is the lowest-rated category across all platforms (3.5/5 on Blind) — not terrible, but a weak spot in an otherwise strong culture
- Talent drain to AI labs and Cursor — if the best engineers are leaving, the remaining team absorbs more load
- Burnout is a recurring theme — "why not today" culture can mean "why not tonight and this weekend too"
- Private equity = illiquid compensation. Until IPO happens, a significant portion of TC is paper value

## Verdict

Notion is one of the stronger late-stage startups to join right now: the product has genuine market traction (100M users, 64% revenue growth), the culture scores well above average, and compensation is top-tier. The main risk is sustainability — the lean team ethos that makes the work high-impact also makes it high-intensity, and the talent drain to AI-native competitors is a real signal. If you're considering Notion, ask about your specific team's attrition in the last 12 months, what the on-call expectations are, and get clarity on the IPO timeline and secondary sale options for your equity. The 3-day in-office requirement in SF is also worth factoring into your decision if you're not local.
